# Artwork and fonts

> **TL;DR:** The app keeps its complete illustrated header library and local typography. Generated illustrations are offered under the project's MIT terms to the extent the author holds rights. Fonts retain their own licenses. Downloaded team logos are excluded.

The twelve headers were generated for this project. Images 01 through 09 were made with ChatGPT; 10 through 12 with Gemini. The public catalog records each packaged filename, generator and SHA-256 hash. The six featured images keep the original manually swipeable carousel and daily starting image. There is no automatic slideshow.

For this public edition, ChatGPT edited 04 and 09 to replace recognizable trophy, uniform, helmet and racing-livery details with original neutral designs. Their compositions and sports remain. No private photos or notification records were used in those edits. The delivered images were converted to WebP without embedded metadata.

The illustrations have project-generated provenance. This is not a claim of exclusive copyright, guaranteed uniqueness, or permission to reuse unrelated third-party marks. Team identity elsewhere in the public app uses names and generic visual treatments.

## Local typography

DM Sans regular, medium and bold identify version 4.004 and the SIL Open Font License in their embedded metadata. Roboto Condensed bold and black identify version 3.008 and Apache 2.0. That Roboto version predates the upstream switch to OFL. The exact original download or subsetting commands for these static font files were not retained.

The complete notices are bundled in `app/src/main/assets/licenses/` and remain part of the standalone APK. Sources for those texts are the pinned official [DM Sans OFL](https://github.com/googlefonts/dm-fonts/blob/4412393b7d2de9fe7a92064c2dce9b5af5d7fd26/Sans/OFL.txt) and [Roboto 3.008 Apache license](https://github.com/googlefonts/roboto-3-classic/blob/8aa699a9a715be7ecf6be41171e2851a580f0fb8/LICENSE.txt).

The README banner is an additional original ChatGPT-generated illustration made for this public release. It contains invented people, notification cards and sports imagery. It is packaged as `docs/images/spoiler-alert-hero.webp` and uses the same project-artwork terms. The original generated file is retained privately for provenance.
